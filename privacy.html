Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Who we are

HypeClips Daily ("we") runs the YouTube channel @hypeclipsdailyhq and the TikTok account @hypeclipsdailyhq. ClipFactory is our internal tool to edit and upload short clips to these accounts. Contact: soloodev97@gmail.com.

Which data ClipFactory uses

ClipFactory only connects to accounts owned by HypeClips Daily. When we connect our own YouTube or TikTok account, the platform issues access tokens that allow ClipFactory to upload videos, read basic profile information of that account and read view counts of our own videos. We do not collect, request or process personal data of any other user.

Storage and sharing

Access tokens are stored only locally on our own computer. They are never sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. Video titles and descriptions may be generated with the help of an AI service from the clip's own content; no personal data of viewers is involved.

Deletion

Access can be revoked at any time in the account settings of Google (myaccount.google.com/permissions) and TikTok (Settings → Security → Apps and services). We then delete the stored tokens.

Content removal

If you appear in one of our clips or own the rights to it and want it removed, write to soloodev97@gmail.com and we will remove it promptly.
